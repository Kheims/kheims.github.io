I'm Djamel, a CIFRE PhD candidate splitting time between [Paris-Saclay](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr), [ISEP](https://www.isep.fr) and [Adservio](https://www.adservio.fr). My thesis is on *automated code quality assessment via collaborative AI*: training and benchmarking deep models (LSTM, GNN, CodeBERT and prompted LLMs) on code smell detection. At Adservio I also work on data engineering and AI products.

Before the PhD I did an engineering degree in electronics at ENP Algiers, then an M.Sc. at Paris-Saclay on information processing and embedded systems.

I am deeply interested in how things work inside and in understanding implementations from first principles. I think this is a must, especially now that agents automate so much away: you have to train your mind to always be able to zoom in.

On the side, I contribute to [mlx-graphs](https://github.com/mlx-graphs/mlx-graphs), a GNN framework built on Apple MLX.

## Teaching

At ISEP I teach *Object-Oriented Programming in Python* (30h, own course materials). At Paris-Saclay, *Modular Programming in C++* and *Databases*.
